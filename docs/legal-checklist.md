# Legal approval gate

This application does not provide legal advice or establish a made-to-order return exemption.

The owner and qualified local counsel must review terms, privacy, shipping, returns/exchanges, accessibility and cookie/analytics consent for the intended Israel launch. Review consumer disclosures, cancellation/refund procedures, invoicing/tax requirements, data retention, marketing consent and official messaging practices.

All policy records start as `draft`. Publish only approved, complete AR/HE/EN versions and retain their approval record. Do not publish empty placeholders or assume translation itself constitutes legal review.
